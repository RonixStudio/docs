<CardGroup cols="2">
  <Card title="**Events that run themselves**" icon="calendar-clock">
    Weekly or one-off schedules in your time zone. A start missed while the server was down opens late with its original end, or is skipped. Warnings before closing, a reminder for players who join during an event, and a boss bar countdown. An open event survives restarts, and you can extend or shorten it while it runs.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**Personal, hidden offers**" icon="package-open">
    Six cards per player in the bundled menu, or as many card slots as you lay out. Rarities with weights, per-set limits and discount ranges; per-item weights, discounts and permissions. `preview` and `odds` show exactly what players can draw before you open the market.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**Rewards of every kind**" icon="sword">
    Vanilla items with full data components: name, lore, enchantments, item model, custom model data, glint, unbreakable, hidden tooltip parts. Any item in `/give` syntax. Custom items from ItemsAdder, Nexo, Oraxen and CraftEngine. Console commands, run exactly once per purchase.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**Vault and PlayerPoints**" icon="tool-case">
    Vault charges through the one economy plugin you name: EssentialsX, CMI, ExcellentEconomy and others. If another plugin takes over Vault, cards become unavailable instead of charging the wrong wallet. Each item picks its currency; PlayerPoints prices are whole points.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**Purchases that never charge twice**" icon="shield-check">
    Every purchase is recorded before and after the charge. If the server crashes mid-purchase, the purchase is held for review instead of retried, the player cannot be charged again, and staff are alerted. Every uncertain case has its own review command.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**A mailbox for full inventories**" icon="mailbox">
    A full inventory does not block a purchase: what does not fit waits in the mailbox, never expires, and is claimed with a click or `/ws mail`. Players with waiting rewards are told when they join.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**Your menus, sounds and words**" icon="languages">
    Menu size, card slots, icons and resource-pack item models. Every sound. Every message in MiniMessage, with English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Russian included.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**PlaceholderAPI**" icon="code-xml">
    Thirteen placeholders for scoreboards, TAB and holograms: open or closed, time left, the next opening, cards revealed and bought, the mailbox count. They read from memory, so they are safe from any thread.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**SQLite or MariaDB/MySQL**" icon="database">
    SQLite with no setup, or MariaDB 10.6+ and MySQL 8.0.16+. A daily SQLite backup, a backup before every schema update, and a cleanup job that never touches an unresolved money record.
  </Card>

  <Card title="**Tools for staff**" icon="slash">
    Status and diagnostics, odds and previews, a player's offers, mailbox and purchases, rerolls, a free `test give` to try rewards before an event, and clickable `/ws help` and `/ws admin help`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
