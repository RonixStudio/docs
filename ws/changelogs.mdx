<Update label="11/10/2026" tags={["RELEASE"]} description="">
  ### **0.1.0 - First Release**

  - **Events:** opened by hand or on a weekly or one-off schedule, with announcements, warnings, a join reminder and a boss bar. An open event survives restarts.
  - **Offers:** a personal, rarity-weighted set per player and event; discounts fixed when the set is made; hidden until revealed.
  - **Currencies:** Vault through one named economy plugin, and PlayerPoints.
  - **Rewards:** vanilla items with full components, custom items (ItemsAdder, Nexo, Oraxen, CraftEngine) and console commands; a mailbox for what does not fit.
  - **Safe purchases:** recorded before and after the charge; never charged twice; uncertain cases held for staff review.
  - Configurable menus, sounds and messages in six languages; PlaceholderAPI; SQLite or MariaDB/MySQL with backups and cleanup.
  - `/ws about`, `/ws admin license`, and clickable `/ws help` and `/ws admin help`.
</Update>
