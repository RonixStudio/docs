<Update label="10/13/2026" tags={["RELEASE"]} description="">
  ### **1.0.0 -** First release.

  - Top navigation with category dropdowns, your own links (**Navbar Links**), a cart button showing the player's skin, and icons for Home, your links and your CMS pages (**Navbar Home Image**, **Page Icons**). On wide screens the menu sits in the header bar beside the logo, closes up when it has more entries, and scrolls sideways when they still do not fit.
  - Category tiles on the home page and, optionally, child categories as tiles on their parent's page (**Subcategories**).
  - Package cards with discount and countdown badges, add-to-cart, gift, the reason a package cannot be bought, `/mo` on subscriptions, and grid or list layout.
  - Packages named like `1 Key`, `5 Keys` can share one card with a quantity switch (**Group Packages by Quantity**, off by default).
  - Package pop-ups with description tabs and options, sized to the screen with the text scrolling inside; Java / Bedrock login, with your server's Floodgate prefix in front of Bedrock players' names (**Bedrock Prefix**).
  - A cart drawer with quantities, currencies, an empty-cart message, and checkout through Tebex Checkout.
  - Server address and Discord blocks with live player and member counts, and a server address window with joining instructions.
  - Announcement bar and a sale banner with a countdown and an optional tag above the headline (**Tag Text**).
  - Modules (featured packages with arrows, top donator, recent payments, gift card, goals, server status, text) in a row under the page or in a sidebar on the left or right (**Module Position**).
  - Vote page (slug `vote`) and Patrons page (slug `patrons`).
  - Rank comparison table with section heading rows and an in-browser editor (paid add-on).
  - Footer with payment logos, social links and a Go top button.
  - License check; the store still opens, without the paid add-ons, when the license server is busy or cannot be reached.
  - Package search: a search box at the top of every page finds any package by name, with its image and price (**Package Search**, on by default).
  - FAQ page: on a CMS page with the slug `faq`, every heading becomes a question that opens to its answer.
  - Purchase notifications: the latest purchases from the Recent Payments module, one at a time in a corner (**Purchase Notifications**, off by default).
  - **Color Preset**: ready-made main colours; a colour set by hand still wins.
  - Seven languages: English, German, Spanish, Turkish, French, Portuguese (Brazil) and Polish; every text the theme adds is translatable.
  - Preview links: a `?ronix_preview=…` link from us opens the theme on a store for a few days without a license, also in Tebex's template preview while another Ronix theme is the active template; trying a theme does not lock the active one.
  - Wishlist: a heart on package cards and in the package window saves packages to a list kept in the visitor's browser; the Wishlist drawer, built like the cart, shows them with an add-to-basket button (**Wishlist**, on by default).
  - Image zoom: images in a package's description open full screen when clicked (**Image Zoom**, on by default).
  - Sticky buy bar: on phones a package's price and add-to-basket button stay in view (**Sticky Buy Bar**, on by default).
  - Reviews on the home page, written in the settings: name, stars and text (**Reviews**, off by default).
  - Your team on the home page, under the reviews: one person at a time with their skin, their role and a line about them, and their heads to switch (**Team**, off by default).
  - A CMS page whose text opens with a heading 1 takes that heading as its title, instead of showing its name above it.
</Update>
