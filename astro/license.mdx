The theme checks its license from your visitors' browsers, against your store's address. You add the license yourself:

1. Link your Discord account to the BuiltByBit account you bought the theme with (on BuiltByBit: **Account → Discord**).
2. Join our Discord ([https://ronix-studio.vercel.app/discord](https://ronix-studio.vercel.app/discord)) and run `/sync` in any channel. You get the customer role for the theme.
3. Open the license panel ([https://ronix-license.vercel.app](https://ronix-license.vercel.app/)), sign in with Discord and add a license: your store's address, as visitors open it (for example `https://store.example.com`), and the theme. A Tebex subdomain and your own domain are two addresses: add a license for each.
4. We approve new licenses by hand. Until then the store shows **"License not approved"**; once it is approved, reload the page. If the message is still there, wait a minute and reload: a browser remembers a refusal for a minute.

What the license controls:

- **Rank table** (`table`): the comparison table on category pages is a paid add-on.
- **Ronix credit** (`trademark`): the license can include removing the Ronix Studios credit from the footer.

Each browser remembers the license answer for 24 hours: a feature added to your license later reaches your visitors within a day (to see it at once yourself, clear your browser's site data for the store). If the license server cannot be reached, is failing or limits the number of checks, the last approval keeps the paid add-ons working for a week; a browser with no approval yet opens the store without them and checks again on the next visit. Only a definite answer closes the store: not approved, rejected, or **"License has been suspended"**.

# **Trying the theme first: preview links**

We can make a **preview link** for your store: a link such as `https://store.example.com/?ronix_preview=…` that opens the theme on that store for a few days, with the add-ons we choose, without a license (and without touching your store's own license). Open the link once; the browser keeps it until it expires. Ask for one on our Discord.

The theme does not have to be your active template. Open Tebex's preview of it (**Webstore → Appearance**), then our link in the same browser; Tebex's preview lasts until you leave it or its link expires. Your visitors keep seeing your active template, and trying a theme does not lock it, even when it is another Ronix theme.
