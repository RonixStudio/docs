  <Tab title="Setup">
    ## **Vote page**

    Create a CMS page (Webstore → Pages) with the slug `vote`. Under **Vote** in the settings, write your rewards (one per line) and your voting site links (one per line); blank lines are ignored.

    ## **Patrons page**

    Create a CMS page with the slug `patrons`. Its content is the page's introduction; any bullet list in it is shown as reward cards. Under **Patrons** in the settings, set up to three tiers: a name, a threshold (such as `$500+`), a badge image and the players, one name per line. A tier without a name is hidden. Long lists show a "View more" button. Each tier is a tile in its own colour (gold, then sky, then green), with the badge on its plate (a crown for the first tier and a star for the others when there is none) and the players' heads under the name; two rows of players show at first, and the button says how many more there are.

    ## **FAQ page**

    Create a CMS page with the slug `faq`. Write each question as a heading (`Heading 2` or `Heading 3` in the editor) and its answer under it: on the store every question opens to its answer when clicked, the first one already open.

    ## **Package search**

    With **Package Search** on (the default), a search box at the top of every page finds any package of the store by name and shows its image and price; a result opens the package. The first search reads your category pages, which can take a moment on a big store; the list is kept for the rest of the visit.

    ## **Purchase notifications**

    Turn on **Purchase Notifications** to show the latest purchases one at a time in the bottom left corner ("Steve purchased VIP"). They come from the **Recent Payments** module, so add that module under **Webstore → Modules**; its own settings decide whether the package name and the time are shown. A visitor can close them for the rest of the visit. Phones do not show them.

    ## **Wishlist**

    With **Wishlist** on (the default), every package card and the package window get a heart. The Wishlist button (in the header, before the cart; on phones, at the end of the menu) shows how many packages are saved and opens the list, with each package's image, its price when it was saved and an add-to-basket button. The list is kept in the visitor's browser, for your store: it does not follow them to another device.

    ## **Image zoom**

    With **Image Zoom** on (the default), the images in a package's description open full screen when clicked; small icons are enlarged without blurring. A click or Escape closes them.

    ## **Sticky buy bar**

    With **Sticky Buy Bar** on (the default), on phones a package's price and its add-to-basket button stay at the bottom of the screen while the description scrolls.

    ## **Reviews**

    Turn on **Reviews** and write them under **Review List**, one per line: the player's name, the stars (1 to 5) and the text, separated by `|`, such as `Notch | 5 | Fast delivery and friendly staff!` (a line with only a name and a text gets 5 stars; do not use `|` in the text). They show on the home page under your description, with the player's head on Minecraft stores, in a row that scrolls sideways when there are more than fit. **Reviews Title** changes the heading (empty: "What players say"). The theme shows what you write; it does not collect reviews.

    ## **Team**

    Turn on **Team** to show your team on the home page, under the reviews: one person at a time, with their skin, their role and a line about them; the arrows and the heads under them switch between them. Write them under **Team Members**, one per line as `Name | Role | Text`, such as `itsSycho | Manager | Runs the team and designs the builds.` (do not use `|` in the text). **Team Title** changes the heading (empty: "Our team"). The skins and heads come from the names, so use the players' Minecraft names; on other games the heads show the name's first letter.

    ## **Bedrock players**

    With **Bedrock** set to `On`, the login window has Java and Bedrock tabs. Tebex runs a package's commands for the name the player logs in with, so a Bedrock player has to log in with the name your server knows them by. Floodgate (Geyser) puts a prefix in front of Bedrock players' names (`username-prefix` in its config, `.` unless you changed it) and turns the spaces in a gamertag into underscores. Set **Bedrock Prefix** to your Floodgate prefix (`None` if you removed it): a Bedrock player who types `Cool Gamer` is then logged in as `.Cool_Gamer`, and the store shows their name without the prefix. Tebex supports Geyser on its Minecraft Offline and Geyser store types; use `{username}` rather than the UUID in your package commands.

    ## **Rank table (paid add-on)**

    The rank table compares the packages of one category perk by perk.

    1. Your license needs the `table` feature. Upload `addons/table.addon.twig` (see Installation) and set **Rank Table** and **Developer Mode** to `On`.
    2. Open the category on your store and click **See Comparison Table**. With Developer Mode on, the editor opens even before the category has a table: add perks, rename them, set each package's value (tick, cross or text), reorder perks and hide packages.
    3. **Sections.** Tick **Section heading** when you add or edit a perk to make it a heading row across the table ("One Time Redeemables", "Perks", "Island Upgrades" …). The perks under it are striped from the heading down.
    4. Click the save button above the table (the clipboard icon, "Copy to clipboard"). It copies one complete line, the category's ID included, such as `{% set rankTable = { 12: { … } } %}`.
    5. In the template editor, open `category.html` and paste the line directly under `{% extends "layout.html" %}`. For a second category, put both tables in the same line: `{% set rankTable = { 12: { … }, 15: { … } } %}`.
    6. Set **Developer Mode** back to `Off`.

    The **Rank Table** settings also set the banner text, an optional title and description shown beside the table, the package to highlight (its package ID) and the highlight badge text.

    ## **Grouping packages by quantity**

    With **Group Packages by Quantity** set to `On` (default: `Off`), packages whose names start with a number and share the rest of the name, such as `1 Crate Key`, `5 Crate Keys` and `10 Crate Keys`, share one card with `1x / 5x / 10x` buttons. Packages without a number keep their own cards.

    ## **Rank upgrades: paying the difference**

    Tebex can charge a player only the difference when they move up to a higher rank. Edit the category your ranks are in (**Webstore → Packages**) and change its type to **Cumulative**: a player who owns a \$10 rank then pays \$20 for a \$30 one, and keeps that discount even after their rank expires. The category can also stop players from buying ranks priced below the one they own. Tebex knows what a player owns once they log in with their name, so the difference applies to logged-in players. The theme needs no setting for this: the category cards keep the full price, while the package window shows a logged-in player their price with the full price struck through, and the cart and Tebex Checkout charge the difference. (Tebex's _Tiered_ categories are for subscriptions and are not available on Minecraft stores.)

    ## **Categories**

    - **Home Page Categories** shows a tile for every category on the home page. A category's image and description (as plain text) appear on its tile.
    - **Subcategories** chooses where child categories go: as tiles on their parent category's page, or in a dropdown in the navbar.
    - Every category page has a **Go back** link to its parent category, or to the home page.

    ## **Menu icons**

    Every entry of the menu can have a small picture in front of its name:

    - **Home**: **Navbar Home Image**.
    - A link from **Navbar Links**: an image URL as the line's third part, such as `Discord | https://discord.gg/abc | https://example.com/discord.png`.
    - A CMS page: a line in **Page Icons**, `page | image URL`, where the page is its address (`faq` for `/faq`) or its title, such as `faq | https://example.com/faq.png`.

    Any image link works: one from an image host, or a picture already on your store (right-click it and copy the image address). Small square PNGs with a transparent background look best.

    ## **Colours**

    **Color Preset** picks a ready-made main colour (`Mint`, `Coral`, `Lilac`, `Orange`, `Grass`). A **Primary Color** you set yourself takes precedence over the preset.

    ## **Layout**

    - **Module Position** (Modules): keep the modules in a row under the page, or show them as a sidebar on the left or right on wide screens. Phones and tablets always show them under the page.
    - The menu: on wide screens its entries sit in the header bar beside the logo. With more entries the bar closes up (the Wishlist button keeps only its heart and the cart moves in); when they still do not fit, they scroll sideways in the bar: a mouse wheel scrolls them too, a fade marks the side that has more, and the current page is brought into view. Phones and tablets get the menu button.
